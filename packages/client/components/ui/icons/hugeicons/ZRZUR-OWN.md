# Icons drawn by Zrzur

These files in this folder are Zrzur's own drawings, not from Hugeicons, and fall under this repository's licence:

- `poll.svg`: three result bars of different lengths, for polls.
- `ticket.svg`: a tilted ticket stub with a notch at each end and three diamonds across it, for tickets.
- `game-controller.svg`: a rounded gamepad with a cross pad and two buttons, for games.
