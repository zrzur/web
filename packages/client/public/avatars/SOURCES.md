# Avatars

The twenty `avatar-*.svg` files and `catalog.json` are the owner's own Zrzur
work: the circle set of "Zrzur Expressive Default Avatars", version 2, built from
Zrzur logo B and copied here unchanged. They are the sample people on Designs
and the default picture for accounts without one.

They carry the Zrzur logo, so they fall under the trademark note in
`assets/README.md`, not under the AGPL-3.0 licence of the code.
